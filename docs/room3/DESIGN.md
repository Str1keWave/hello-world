# DESIGN — v3 bible (showrunner synthesis)

## Rival assemblies, ruled

**A (room consensus):** the MIMIC — words arrive late, pried from the
creature's own camouflage, each spend permanent. **B (Puppeteer's floor):**
the entire mandate in body language alone; "talking" stays gestural forever
(Skeptic's goodbye pitch needs no words). Ruling: **A, under B's
discipline.** The audience asked for "eventually trying to TALK"; A's
speech-costs-cover mechanic is the substance engine v1 never had, and the
climax ("understood cold") needs words. But B governs dosage: the creature
is pre-verbal for most of the session, words stay scarce, physical,
effortful, and every trap in the brief is a trap of words. If a beat works
without a word, it gets no word.

## THE ANIMAL (one anatomy, room-fused)

The page IS the creature. A cephalopod-like animal whose body is the
viewport and whose camouflage posture is a slightly-wrong municipal
tide-table page. The same skin that hides is the organ that speaks (true
of real cephalopods). Its drive is camouflage-safety; contact with the
visitor gradually outbids it. Speaking = prying words out of its own
disguise; every pried word leaves a permanent visible gap with one
consistent authored tooth-mark (kerned bite). Courage is measurable in
gaps.

## THE STAGE (the camouflage posture — authored copy)

**PORT SORREL — TIDE TABLES** (the real town is Port Sorell; one L is the
slightly-wrongness). Contents: a seven-day tide table with columns
PREDICTED and OBSERVED (heights, times), moon phase line, water conditions
line ("slack at 14:20", "still"), a notice box (harbour office hours), nav
("Home"), last-updated line, footer ("© Port Sorrel Harbour Authority.
All rights reserved."). Styling: honest municipal-web (system fonts, a
table, one blue). Every word of copy is chosen to be prieable vocabulary.

**The lexicon** (word → its overextended meaning when spoken):
- slack — stillness / peace / the goodbye word
- observed — you-are-real / I-see-you (vs "predicted" = imagined)
- predicted — discarded at midpoint, visibly, in favor of observed
- high water — joy / much / yes
- low water — sad / little
- first light — beginning / hello
- reserved — mine / stop / boundary
- home — here / safe
- hours — time / waiting
- PORT SORREL (the header) — its name; spent only at the climax

## THE BODY (Puppeteer's six, in cephalopod anatomy)

- **Breathe:** mantle-swell — a slow whole-skin luminance/scale breath,
  always on (reduced-motion: opacity only, slower).
- **Startle (one reflex):** fast flick / hard tap → snap to full boring-page
  mimicry in one frame, then recovery with residue: table columns fractionally
  misaligned + fast breathing, decaying. Habituates: repeated harmless
  startles get shorter recoveries. Sensitization after the Break: thresholds
  tighten again.
- **Wave (arousal):** cuttlefish passing-clouds — soft chromatic bands
  crossing the page, rate = arousal.
- **Papillae (Grain):** slow strokes raise a ripple that leans toward the
  finger, trailing afterglow adjacent to (never under) the thumb. One
  region (the notice box) is the permanent no: it flattens and dulls every
  time, never explained.
- **Echo, then ask:** tap-motifs echoed as subsurface pulse-trains; drag
  motifs as papillae paths retracing the touch line, imperfect (poor
  proprioception is the alibi). Later: varies (your rhythm + one beat,
  offered). Later: initiates a motif YOU invented, and waits.
- **The glyph (play):** its grooming-object — a loose pried letterform it
  noses around. Keepaway/possession-swap play loop, uninstructed. It gives
  the glyph to you mid-arc (the carried gift). Wrong Guess lives inside
  play: it anticipates your throws, overcommits, slinks. Baits capped at 2.
- **Held Breath (the intimacy channel and every cadence-rest):** ~2s
  still-hold onset → breath under the finger syncs → approach → full weight
  against the finger. All speech beats begin from held breath.

## THE ARC (action-gated; no clocks anywhere)

Escalation axis: EXPOSURE. Ladder: gesture trust → motifs → ratified words
→ revocable speech → permanent spends → the name. 7–9 cadence-rests
(graded Held Breath), never more than ~90 seconds of interaction apart;
any tab-close after any rest reads as an ending. Blur/lock resumes at the
last rest, animate.

- **Movement 1 — NERVES.** Startle economy + Grain teach gentleness as the
  only verb. Threshold: N gentle events after last startle.
- **Movement 2 — PLAY.** The glyph appears; keepaway; wrong guesses. Motif
  bank fills (tap-rhythms, drag-shapes). Threshold: first completed
  possession-swap.
- **Movement 3 — ECHO→ASK.** Echoes become variations become its first
  initiation (your old motif, performed at you). Ostension begins: it
  points (mote of attention) at words; touching the word ratifies it into
  the shared lexicon. Nothing enters its language unratified.
- **Movement 4 — SLACK (first word, revocable).** In held-breath stillness
  it rehearses in skin (subsong: almost-glyph shimmer), then lifts "slack"
  off the conditions line and offers it. The gap behind it is pale — this
  word is still repossessable: a startle here and it slams the word back in
  (era of revocable speech).
- **Movement 5 — THE MIDPOINT SPEND.** It prises OBSERVED from the table
  header and — deliberately, watched — discards "predicted" off-viewport.
  This gap is permanent: first tooth-mark scar. Meaning lands by use, not
  recap: it uses observed AT you (your ratified word + observed).
- **Movement 6 — THE BREAK.** During play, your rewarded winning move
  collides with the carried gift-glyph (watched causality: only a true
  witnessed collision fires it — never speed, never a graze). The glyph
  shatters. Its re-camouflage reflex fires and HALF-FAILS: the posture
  snaps back but flickers against its permanent gaps — it can no longer
  fully hide, and you watch it discover that. Sensitization: thresholds
  tighten. Responsiveness floor holds (flinch within one touch; never
  inert). Repair: Movement-1 verbs only — slow strokes, stillness — each
  mended shard re-earns presence; legible progress within two touches;
  bounded by actions, not time.
- **Movement 7 — FLUENCY (ceiling).** Post-repair it speaks in the earned
  register — found-telegraphese, tide-grammar, overextensions kept ("high
  water" for every joy). Sample ceiling lines (Poet's, the only authored
  lines): "observed. you also observed." / "high water. all of it high
  water." / "page not found. found." Its wit is late and paid for by
  witnessed failure.
- **Movement 8 — THE NAME (climax).** From full weight against your held
  finger, it spends the header. PORT SORREL lifts, breaks into letters,
  re-forms at your fingertip as its offering — the page above now headless,
  the posture unrecoverable, speech as the final layer of camouflage
  surrendered. It asks nothing back. (If the visitor has established a
  name-motif of their own, it echoes that after — never required.)
- **Movement 9 — IT SAYS GOODBYE FIRST.** It composes the goodbye from
  ONLY gestures and words the visitor ratified or invented; the last word
  is "slack", returned gently to the empty conditions line, where it fits
  again. Then it withdraws — warmly, deliberately, first — into what
  camouflage it has left. The page rests: calm, quiet, gaps unhealed.
  Acceptance test: a screenshot of the goodbye must mean nothing to anyone
  whose thumb didn't build it.

**Return visit:** one recognition gesture in the shared language (its
quickest breath-sync ever, plus your first-ratified word pulsed once).
Nothing more. Never required, never rewarded beyond recognition.

## LAWS (Skeptic — binding, adopted by the whole room)

All 15 traps + 9 phone truths + the 8 FINAL rulings, verbatim from
docs/room3/pitches/skeptic.md. Non-negotiables restated: no narration of
perception anywhere, ever (delete-test governs all copy); responses land
adjacent to contact, meaning rides the wavefront; ~2s hold onset with
long-press/selection/callout suppression; noise floor under the startle
(graze travel/duration threshold); every word-spend on-screen, mid-viewport,
in the region of recent contact, blur-mid-spend rewinds; state persists per
ratified event, restores animate; transform/opacity only, rAF, 60fps or the
fiction dies; no meters, no recaps, no keyboards; interruption absorbed
silently — first touch back is met a little quicker.

## BUILD PLAN

- Rendering: DOM tide-table page (real, selectable, boring) + one
  full-viewport canvas UNDER the text for skin (coarse chromatophore cell
  grid, ~24×44, gradient cells, upscaled; passing-clouds as band shaders;
  breath as global luminance) + a small overlay layer for the glyph, the
  attention-mote, and airborne words (DOM spans, FLIP transforms).
- Modules: skin.js (canvas fields), nerves.js (arousal/trust/habituation
  state machine), touch.js (gesture classification, noise floor, motif
  bank), play.js (glyph physics + keepaway + wrong-guess predictor),
  language.js (ostension, ratification, spends, gaps, register lines),
  arc.js (movement thresholds, cadences, break/repair, endgame), stage.js
  (the tide-table DOM + word inventory), persist.js (per-event state,
  animate restore; localStorage only — no cookies, no IndexedDB: this
  creature keeps no records, only thresholds).
- The old v1/v2 code is torn out of public/ entirely (docs stay). New page,
  new everything. Deploy restores the Pages workflow to serve public/ only
  when QA is green end-to-end.
- prefers-reduced-motion: breath→opacity, clouds→slow fades, startle→cut,
  play speeds halved; full arc still completable.
- Debug (?debug=1 only): state readout + movement-skip buttons; console
  otherwise ships empty.
