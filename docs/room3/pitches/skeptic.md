# THE SKEPTIC — Trap Map, Phone Truths, Two Pitches

## 1. THE TRAP MAP (kill-criteria — a pitch that trips one of these is dead)

1. **Overtriggering.** Every touch spawns a reaction; the queue of clever boxes returns. v1 died of this in a day.
2. **Narration in a fur coat.** "You're scrolling fast" becomes "it notices your haste" — still narration, still v1's disease.
3. **Asserted aliveness.** Any copy that says "I am awake / I can see you." Aliveness is a behavior budget, not a script line.
4. **The stutter reel.** Glitch text, "H-hello?", zalgo, jump scares, "I've been waiting for you." Pre-convicted by the brief; still someone will pitch it wearing a hat.
5. **Waiting in costume.** Shyness cooldowns, "it needs to rest," slow-fade timers longer than ~10 seconds. Any mechanic where the optimal move is to do nothing is v2.
6. **Front-loaded reveal.** The best trick fires in minute 2, then 28 minutes of plateau. v1's reveal-then-repeat corpse.
7. **No ending, only a plateau.** The arc runs out of escalation and loops. Rooms under-write endings; this room owes one that is played, not watched.
8. **Idle-cursor dependence.** Any beat that needs a resting cursor, hover, or mouse trail. On the canonical stage those senses do not exist.
9. **Memory as dossier.** "It remembers you visited 3 times" / choice recaps / anything shaped like a record. Memory may only show as changed behavior, never as recall.
10. **Fake-broken theater.** Distorted UI, error screens, frozen states. On a phone this reads as an actual crash; the one audience member bounces and never learns it was art.
11. **Quips that don't accumulate.** Reactive one-offs with no grammar carrying forward. Substance = earlier interactions change the meaning of later ones.
12. **Tamagotchi economics.** Meters, feeding, needs, care chores. A creature that bills you is a pet, not a presence.
13. **The jaded-AI voice.** Weary, sardonic, too-cool narrator. The audience named it as a cliché; it will sneak back in as "tone."
14. **Text-input dependence.** A keyboard eats half the 390×844 stage and breaks the spell. If it talks, the visitor must be able to answer without typing.
15. **Mechanics as decoration.** A story that could be a video with the interactions removed. If the interaction is skippable, the pitch is a short film, not a THING.

## 2. THE PHONE TRUTHS (any pitch must survive all of these)

1. **Touch occludes.** The finger hides the exact pixels it touches. Responses must appear adjacent to contact, never under it.
2. **Fingers are fat.** ~44px minimum targets, no precision gestures, no pixel-hunting. The creature must read intent generously.
3. **Absence is ambiguous.** No cursor means no idle signal. Not-touching could be reading, thinking, or gone. The creature cannot treat silence as data with confidence — and must not stall waiting for input that isn't coming.
4. **Interruption is normal, not dramatic.** Notifications, app switches, screen lock mid-moment. Every state must survive a 2-minute blur/focus cycle without breaking, punishing, or commenting.
5. **Scroll is sacred.** Hijacked or janky scroll reads as broken. If scroll is a sense, it must still behave like scroll. Browser chrome also resizes the viewport as it hides/shows — layouts must not jump.
6. **One thumb, bottom third.** Sustained interaction happens in the thumb arc. Anything critical parked in the top corners costs a grip change; the creature should come to the thumb, not summon the thumb.
7. **Jank reads as dead.** Mid-range phone, 60fps or the illusion of life dies instantly. rAF, transforms only, no layout thrash — this is a fiction constraint, not just a perf one.
8. **Sound is off, screen is dim.** 11pm context: muted phone, low brightness, low patience. Audio can carry nothing essential; low-contrast subtlety will simply not be seen.
9. **Noise happens.** Palm brushes, edge-swipes, pocket touches. One accidental graze must not fire a story beat or spend an escalation.

## 3. TWO PITCHES

### Pitch A — It Says Goodbye First (the ending)

The whole arc is teaching each other a touch language — its taps and drifts, your echoes, until specific rhythms carry agreed meanings. The ending: the creature uses only gestures the visitor has already used or answered — no new text, no speech unlock, no lore dump — to say the one thing it has been building toward, and then ends the conversation itself. It withdraws deliberately, warmly, first. No fade-to-black cutscene; the visitor's own hands are in it to the last beat. Afterward the page is genuinely quiet — calm, not broken. A returning visitor gets one recognition gesture in the shared language and nothing more. The test: could the ending be screenshotted and understood? If yes, it failed — it must only mean something to the person whose thumb built it.

### Pitch B — Conversation at Thumb Height (the mechanic)

The creature's entire sensorium is what a phone truly has: where the thumb rests, rhythm of taps, direction and speed of a drag. Its replies always land adjacent to contact — around the finger, never under it — and inside the thumb arc, so the exchange feels close, at whisper distance. It echoes your rhythm with slight variation; later it initiates a rhythm and leaves a beat of space (seconds, never longer) for you to echo back. Meaning accumulates by use, not announcement: a rhythm becomes yes; a slow drag becomes come here. Mid-arc it asks its first question in that language, answerable only in that language — no keyboard, no buttons, no narration anywhere. Blur and lock are absorbed silently; your first touch back is simply met a little quicker.

## **FINAL** — Revised kill-criteria (post-discussion)

The room converged on MIMIC + Echo-Then-Ask + MAGPIE (page as camouflaged body; speech by prying words from its own disguise). The map, updated by what the discussion exposed:

1. All 15 original traps stand. New, binding additions:
2. **Unwitnessed damage is a bug.** Every word-spend happens on-screen, inside the region of recent contact, mid-viewport, with one authored gap-signature. Blur mid-spend rewinds or re-presents.
3. **Eviction is the real reload.** iOS kills backgrounded tabs. State persists on every ratified event (never on unload); restore opens animate, never as a cold gapped page.
4. **Hiding must stay animate.** Withdrawal/re-camouflage keeps a responsiveness floor — flinch within one touch, repair legible within two, regression bounded by action, not time.
5. **Use, not recap.** Learned words are deployed at the visitor in present tense; no portrait, no rendered vocabulary, no meters.
6. **Long-press is enemy territory.** Held-finger intimacy suppresses callout/selection/magnifier; signals bloom around contact, meaning in the wavefront.
7. **Noise floor.** Grazes below a travel/duration threshold are not perceived.
8. **Ending acceptance test:** a screenshot of the goodbye must mean nothing to anyone whose thumb didn't build it.
