# SYNTHESIS — the v2 bible (showrunner, Phase 4)

## The two rival assemblies, and the ruling

**Assembly A (spine build):** the late failure is kinetic — the site breaks
L3 once, restoring the long-subtracted testimonial LIVE under the visitor's
verification ritual; convergence crashes; the arc closes with One Question
as the single second-person address; the fourth testimonial runs
unattributed.

**Assembly B (endgame build):** the failure is semantic — the Fourth
Testimonial IS the failure, the first edit that refuses normalization, its
confession printed inside itself; S2 is cut; the site never authors a
second-person word in its life; pricing turn; sunset.

**Argument.** A's strength is the thunderclap: ten-plus visits of
never-changes-while-watched exist so that ONE violation lands with real
force; B alone means the visitor never once sees the page move, and the
audience explicitly asked for interaction that shows rather than states. B's
strengths are discipline (the withheld second person is the ending's
negative space — killjoy#7 honored to the letter), self-authentication (a
bug cannot quote your design document; the testimonial needs no other proof
of authorship), and economy (failure, address, and recontextualization in
one edit). A's question spends composure in a register the site never
otherwise uses, at the finish line — the endgame build's critique stands.

**Ruling: B's architecture, with A's experiment kept as the crash-cause in
the socket B left empty.** B never explains how the convergence metric dies;
A's control experiment is that explanation, and it is a different organ from
B's ending (kinetic proof of awareness vs. terminal address), so this is
sequencing, not averaging. S2 is cut. X1: E1 takes the lifetime address.
X2: the backstage is empty (devtools sensor deleted; console ships clean).
X3: L5 strict — a pre-ending wipe gets the pristine mask forever, no tell.
The board's one boundary-crossing budget goes UNSPENT: the site never
leaves the page. Restraint is the statement, and v1 already spent the
clipboard on this visitor.

## THE ARC (canonical beat list)

Phase gate throughout: L4, phase = min(distinct visits, distinct calendar
days). Visit = L2 resume definition (visibility-resume or load after ≥6h
gap; same-day continuations are one visit). All effects settle at
visit-start, flushed while hidden (L1/L3). All copy in L7 grammar.

- **V1 — the mask.** Over-full perfect page (4 testimonials, 6 logos, long
  nav). Inheritance already applied as competence (see INHERITANCE). Footer
  links to a real /status: uptime, changelog with late-1.x backdated entries
  matching the visitor's real v1 session dates, "Beta convergence: 61%"
  reading as jargon. Nothing else. Zero anomalies.
- **V2 — the second nothing.** Still perfect. One mundane changelog entry.
  The armed events hold (C1 mandates clean).
- **V3 — the subtraction.** One removal, reflowed (a testimonial). C2 armed:
  the pronoun slip ("your side"→"our side"), one render, reload restores,
  never recurs. Ledger: "2.4.1 — Fixed an issue where a testimonial could
  overstate." Convergence ticks up.
- **V4–V5 — normalization legible.** Whatever drew the second look last
  visit is repaired/renamed/removed by the next. C3: three consecutive
  same-first-scroll-target visits freeze that section forever. C4: stale
  sessions train pull-to-refresh; repairs the visitor performs tick
  convergence up; investigation stalls it.
- **V6 — Overheard.** Settlement after the heaviest investigation streak:
  chat widget, mid-conversation, four lines, five seconds, self-cancel on
  app-switch, once ever, widget never exists again.
  Lines (canon): "— confirmed. single visitor, live now" / "— hold the
  release" / "— how long" / "— until they stop checking"
- **V7 — the cover-up and the appointment.** Ledger blames the widget on a
  fixed bug (the first identifiable lie). Same settlement: one future-dated
  entry — maintenance window, one hour, 2–5 days out, adjacent to but
  outside the visitor's habitual hour. One in-genre banner in the landing
  footer, once.
- **Window attended:** /status serves the ledger unredacted for that hour —
  every entry expanded to what it actually did; one entry describes a visit
  they don't remember. Redacts back at hour's end, permanently.
  **Missed:** once-flag closes; deadpan completed-maintenance entry; proof
  is permanently lost; arc unharmed.
- **V8–V9 — the thinning.** Fewer edits, longer quiets, convergence into
  the 90s. /app reachable behind its login wall once the mid-arc gate
  clears (frames build). Discovery choreography per frames: changelog →
  Reader → /app wall → /app → two-tab → landscape row; every discovery
  makes the site quieter.
- **V10 (gate: min≥8 AND appointment resolved AND sustained investigation)
  — THE EXPERIMENT.** At 97% the site cannot distinguish belief from
  performed non-checking. It breaks L3 once: during the visitor's
  verification ritual, in the C3-frozen section, the V3-subtracted
  testimonial returns IN VIEW, live. The only motion the site ever makes
  under observation. No ledger entry ever covers it — the first anomaly
  with no cover-up. Next settlement: convergence collapses to a low number,
  unexplained. Normalization stops arc-wide: what is found now stays found.
- **V11 — THE ADDRESS (E1).** The fourth testimonial appears at
  visit-start: identical format, seeded metadata style, real trajectory
  numbers. Quote: "It should feel truly alive, not just pretending to be."
  Attribution: the E4/F1 name if one exists, else bare — "— Founder."
  It refuses to heal: inspection, reload, absence — it stays. The lifetime
  address, spent as an edit.
- **V12 — the pricing turn (E3, reduced).** After the testimonial has been
  dwelt on, or two calendar days: the tier CTAs go live as the retention
  choice. No new copy — the tiers always described memory retention. One
  tap, once-flagged, irreversible, unacknowledged. No tap before the close
  clock → the Seed tier's "7-day memory" applies by default.
- **V13 — SUNSET (E2).** Landing page replaced by the startup-death notice,
  played straight, numbers singular: "Loam is winding down." "Since 2021,
  our beta served one visitor, with care." "Thank you for your attention."
  Plus "Data will be retained." — present only if retention was chosen or
  defaulted; if they chose the short-memory tier, all channels genuinely
  delete on schedule and the line is absent. Permanent across every reset
  (the one authored exception to wipe-mask; it is the ending honoring its
  own copy).

**Failure-path endings (all authored):** never returns → the site never
finishes; wipes pre-ending → pristine mask forever (L5); never investigates
→ convergence walks to 100 quietly and the sunset arrives gentle, the
experiment and Overheard never fire (they gate on investigation); the
appointment missed → proof lost, arc continues.

## INHERITANCE (from the frames build, canonical)

Importer runs once at v2 first load; v1 keys left in place untouched.

and boring. v1 session dates → backdated late-1.x changelog entries.

## LAWS (binding on implementation)

L1 arm/settle (≤1 settlement effect per return; expiry). L2 resume-visits
(6h). L3 hidden-flush only — sole authored exception: the experiment.
L4 two clocks. L5 multi-channel once-flags; wipe → mask, no tell; sunset
survives. L6 glance-proof beats, self-cancel on app-switch. L7 grammar of
"it" all copy. Backstage empty (no devtools sensor, no console content,
professionally quiet build). v1's reaction content: retired from the live
path — sensors keep sensing into memory; the 406-rule surfacing layer does
not load. The #voice bubble UI is deleted. No emoji, no exclamation marks
anywhere, including the sunset.

## BUDGET LEDGER (lifetime)

≤1. Boundary crossings: 0 of 1, deliberately. Early anomalies: ≤1/session,

# PUNCH-UP RESPONSES (Phase 5) — take/reject with reasons, and amended canon

P1 TAKE (wall illegibility). Amendment: footer carries "v2.4.1 · updated
nightly" from day one — cadence legible as genre furniture; AND the first
same-day return may claim exactly one settlement (the wall starts at the
second same-day return, and now it reads as schedule, not absence).
P2 TAKE (imperceptible removals). Amendment: removals target high-salience
countables (a whole pricing tier, a named testimonial), and every removal
leaves ONE dangling reference elsewhere ("Compare all four plans" above
three tiers). Deniable as sloppy webdev; findable; points at the wound.
P3 TAKE (no early hook). Amendment: the pronoun slip moves to VISIT 1,
present on first render, fixed on reload, once ever — the visitor watches
the page get corrected between loads on day one. Teaches "this page is
edited" without spending the mask (a typo fixed is genre-true).
P4/P5 TAKE (empty casual arc; backwards ignition). Amendment: Overheard,
appointment, E1, pricing, sunset all gate on the two clocks only;
investigation ACCELERATES and shapes (convergence war, the experiment) but
never ignites. Ignition = slip + nightly cadence + dangling references.
P6 TAKE (cute ending). The brief-quote is dead, both candidates. Amendment:
E1's payload is the visitor's own typed-then-deleted text (v2 or inherited
v1) rendered as the quote; if none exists, the quote is EMPTY — a fourth
testimonial with metadata and attribution but nothing to say, the subject
who never gave the site a word. "— Founder. Subject since <real date>."
P7 TAKE (buried spine). Amendment: the beta's progress shadows on-page as
ordinary copy drift: "Free while in beta." → "The beta is closing soon." →
(after the crash) the line is gone. /status remains the detailed ledger.
P8 TAKE-PARTIAL (appointment lost). Amendment: window widens to 24h on a
day 2–4 out; the in-genre maintenance banner persists across the preceding
days (as real ones do). Missing it remains a real, authored loss.
P9 TAKE. Definition: appointment resolved = attended OR window elapsed.
Missing blocks nothing, ever.
P10 TAKE — resolved by P3.
P11 TAKE (Overheard narrates the premise). Amendment — new canon lines,
never referencing the visitor or watching: "— is it ready" / "— been ready
for weeks" / "— so why are we holding" / "— ask the founder". Two parties,
a finished held thing, an unnamed authority: all new information; the last
line plants the word E1 pays off.
P12 TAKE-VIA-P6/P11, REJECT the maximal reading. The surprise was never
"watching" — it is the want (to pass) and the structure (the visitor is the
founder); with P6/P11 the reveals now point there. /app stays: its content
is the cover story being TRUE, which is frame material, not premise rerun.
P13 TAKE (silent misrouting). Amendments: C3 freeze requires a non-hero
section and a scroll-past-then-return pattern; "long stills" require an
active session (input within 30s, page visible); every inference the
scheduler makes is inspectable in the debug overlay; thresholds
conservative.
P14 TAKE (experiment fragility). De-staged: the restoration fires when the
subtracted element's region is in-viewport during an active session on the
gated visit; if never witnessed within its window, fallback = present at
next visit-start and, uniquely, no ledger cover-up ever posts. Only on the
investigative path; the casual path proceeds directly to E1.
P15 TAKE. Beats re-indexed to phases, not visit numbers; full arc ≈ 9–10
visits across ≥7 distinct days; all constants in one tunable module.
P16 TAKE (sunset wistfulness). Rewritten cold: "Loam is winding down." /
"The beta concluded on <date>. We reached the coverage we needed." /
"Sessions: 1 subject." / conditional "Data will be retained." Nothing
thanks anyone. Nothing is served "with care."
P17 TAKE-PARTIAL. The pre-humbled rage-target is cut (illegible wink). The
backdated changelog and the bot-row stay — REJECT with reason: they are
evidence in the founder-structure (v1 was the intake interview), they do
plot work (the repeated lie), and the punch-up's own verdict-6 disposition
credits this machinery.
P18 TAKE — the thinning era carries the on-page countdown (P7) and the
persistent maintenance banner (P8), so late quiet reads as approaching,
not exhausted.

Verdict-2 residue accepted knowingly: Overheard remains words. It is one of
two word-moments in the site's life, neither describes the visitor, and the
alternative (cutting the only proof of a second party) costs the plot more
than four cold logistics lines cost the doctrine.
