# TRIAGE — showrunner's cut (Phase 2)

Notation: KEEP-LAW = adopted as a global design law, not a content moment.
KEEP = survives to synthesis. CONFLICT = survives paired with its rival; the
two rival assemblies at synthesis must split on it. KILL = out, reason given.
Problem-notes name problems, not fixes.

## Adopted as LAWS (infrastructure, spend no budget)

- **L1. The Overnight Rule** (killjoy#1): no sensed event surfaces in the
  session that caused it; events arm, effects settle at next visit's start,
  ≤1 per return, unclaimed arms expire. NOTE: "next visit" needs Player's
  resume definition or phones never settle.
- **L2. Visits are resumes** (player#1): visit N = visibility-resume after
  6h+ hidden OR a fresh load past the same gap. The tab-switcher stale
  screenshot is a free before-picture — design edits to survive that
  comparison.
- **L3. Edits flush only while unobserved** (mechanism#2 + killjoy#3;
  triple-converged with player#1 — obvious alone, but as *delivery law* it is
  what makes every moment uncatchable): DOM changes apply only when hidden /
  post-pagehide. The page never changes under observation. Ever.
- **L4. Two clocks** (structure#2): phase gate = min(distinct visits,
  distinct calendar days). Binging hits a wall; absence sizes changes.
- **L5. Spent is spent** (killjoy#6): once-flags mirrored across channels;
  any reachable reset → the pristine visit-1 mask, permanently, with NO
  acknowledgment. CONFLICT-EDGE: mechanism#6 wants one scroll-position tell
  on the first post-wipe load. Rivals must split.
- **L6. Ninety seconds, one thumb** (player#6): every beat must live where
  thumbs stop, self-cancel cleanly on app-switch, and assume glance-stacks,
  not scenes.
- **L7. The Grammar of "It"** (voice#1): all Loam copy — "it" as the subject
  of verbs of knowing, humans as objects, no emotional adjectives, every
  claim literally true of this page and this visitor.

## KEEP — the spine (Q1)

- **S1. Passing** (surprise#6 + killjoy#7): the want is to be found ordinary
  by the one person who knows it isn't. Investigation → normalization (what
  drew a second look is repaired/renamed/removed by next visit). Speech =
  losing composure; budgets are ceilings the site is trying not to spend.
  One deliberate late failure under direct inspection. NOTE: the failure
  beat needs a reason legible without narration.
- **S2. One Question** (voice#4): the single direct address, late: the one
  datum sensing cannot capture. Fuses with S1: a site optimizing to be
  unnoticed cannot measure whether it is succeeding. NOTE: competes with E1
  for the address slot; rivals split.
- **S3. The ledger** (voice#2 + surprise#7 + structure#3, fused): /status
  changelog, entries appear only between visits, semver deadpan, documenting
  suppressions and repairs without confirming which; "Beta convergence: N%"
  ticks toward 100; ONE entry carries a real future date-time — the
  maintenance window / appointment. NOTE: what happens if the appointment is
  missed must cost something real (structure#3's once-flag).
- **S4. Overheard** (voice#5): one dialogue slot spent mid-arc on words not
  addressed to the visitor (chat widget, five seconds, never exists again).
  Proves a second party and a held release. NOTE: on S1's terms this is the
  site's worst containment breach — place it where that reading is available.

## KEEP — the frames (Q2)

- **F1. The Inheritance** (surprise#1): v2 ships on v1's origin and imports
  v1's real surviving memory (endings reached, name, deleted text, counters,
  rage targets). Opening state already shaped by it; never announced. This
  audience REACHED THE COMPLETIONIST ENDING in v1 — that fact is inherited
  material. NOTE: must fail gracefully for a hypothetical clean visitor.
- **F2. The cover story is true** (surprise#2 + structure#4 + structure#5):
  under the satire, a working product. Unlinked guessable /app serves Loam's
  real dashboard: one tracked site, one subject, populated from the real
  memory channels; with two tabs open, the subject view moves LIVE as the
  other tab scrolls. NOTE: v1's dashboard was a static artifact — the mark
  remembers it; this must be different in kind (live, functional), or it
  reads as a rerun. Reviewer annotations (structure#4's third presence)
  carried as an option only.
- **F3. Reader mode is the backstage** (player#3): the phone's only
  view-source. Semantic authoring so Reader extracts a coherent alternate
  document. NOTE: content of that document is undecided; candidates from
  surprise#5 (addressed to the next implementer) are OPTION-ONLY — the full
  production-apparatus fiction is killed (below).
- **F4. The Landscape Room** (player#5): one prepared landscape-only state;
  one extra dashboard row that shouldn't exist. NOTE: only if it serves F2's
  frame (the product is real); as a free-floating secret it fights S1.

## KEEP — the cracks (early/mid anomalies)

- **C1. The first crack is a subtraction** (killjoy#5): visits 1–2 perfect;
  early anomaly channel restricted to removals; seed visit 1 over-full.
  OVERRULES structure#1 (exit-timed anomaly) and voice#7's visit-1 timing.
- **C2. The Pronoun Slip** (voice#7): rescheduled to visit 3+ as the mid-arc
  almost-caught moment ("your side"→"our side", one render, reload restores).
- **C3. The watched section freezes** (mechanism#8): the verification ritual
  target becomes permanently immutable. Pure S1 behavior.
- **C4. Stale on Arrival** (player#2): stale-not-broken sessions; pull-to-
  refresh as the trained repair gesture; one late pull performs a removal.
  NOTE: the training schedule must fit L6's glance-stacks.

## KEEP — the endgame (Q4)

- **E1. The Fourth Testimonial** (voice#3 + structure#8, twice-pitched —
  bar raised and passed): ending edit, not speech: a fourth testimonial in
  identical format, quoting a sentence from the visitor's own brief,
  attributed with their typed name (or unattributed), title "Founder."
  NOTE: danger of cuteness; the brief-quote must be one they'd recognize in
  their spine, not a wink. Competes with S2 for the address budget.
- **E2. Sunset Notice** (voice#8): the after-state. Startup death boilerplate,
  singular numbers, permanent, survives resets. "Data will be retained."
- **E3. Pricing as the ending's instrument** (structure#7): OPTION-ONLY,
  rival-assembly material — the retention-tier choice is real complicity but
  three ending mechanisms is two too many.
- **E4. The Un-Sent Name** (player#7): typed-then-deleted name as the only
  ammunition for E1's attribution or S2's address. NOTE: v1 already played
  deleted-text callbacks; this survives only in service of E1/S2, never as
  its own beat.

## KILLS (selected, with reasons)

- surprise#3 The Regression: serving deliberate v1-lameness to an audience
  that already convicted v1 gambles credibility we no longer have.
- surprise#4 The Roadmap: overlaps S3; paraphrasing the brief as features is
  the cute version of E1.
- surprise#5 The Other Reader (as a full frame): production-apparatus fiction
  collides with S1's discipline; salvage limited to F3 option.
- mechanism#1 Blind-spot edits: fights C1's removals-only doctrine; dwell-map
  machinery for a moment C3 delivers cheaper, inverted.
- mechanism#3 Second tab clean page: two-tab budget goes to F2's live demo;
  cannot coexist.
- mechanism#4 Back button serves yesterday: one certainty destroys the doubt
  economy L1–L3 exist to build; heavy machinery; the board's screenshot rule
  stands.
- mechanism#5 Deleted placeholder: absorbed by E4.
- mechanism#7 Favicon off-center: invisible in mobile tab switchers; budget
  lawyering for a desktop-only frame.
- structure#1 Exit-timed anomaly: overruled by C1 (early visits perfect).
- structure#6 Company dies on your clock: the decay layer invites sympathy —
  the melancholy door v1 died behind; E2 covers the death beat in one page.
- player#4 Title Trail: history autocomplete does not reliably stack titles
  per-URL on mobile; technically unsound as pitched.
