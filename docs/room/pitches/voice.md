# VOICE PITCHES — the few words that survive

Note on budgets: pitches 3, 4, 5, and 6 each spend ONE dialogue-class slot and
compete — they do not sum. Pitches 3 and 4 also compete for the single direct
address. Pitches 1, 2, 7, and 8 are ordinary page copy and spend nothing.

---

## 1. The Grammar of "It"

- **Name:** The Grammar of "It"
- **Mechanism:** Voice rules for ALL ordinary Loam copy, applied now, in every
  phase. Rules: the product is always "it," always the grammatical subject of
  verbs of knowing; humans are always objects; "we" appears only for logistics
  (pricing, setup, legal); no emotional adjectives anywhere; and every
  marketing claim must be literally true of this page acting on this visitor.
- **The words:** "It reads hesitation the way you read tone." / "Loam doesn't
  sample. Sampling means deciding some moments don't matter." / Keep, verbatim:
  "We'll never share your data. It never even leaves this page."
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** The satire never claims feeling —
  the copy is a spec sheet that the ending re-reads as sworn testimony.
- **Answers:** Q4

---

## 2. Release Notes

- **Name:** Release Notes
- **Mechanism:** The /status page grows a changelog. Entries appear only
  between visits, in strict semver deadpan, third person, documenting edits
  the visitor half-remembers but cannot verify. One number ticks upward
  across the arc.
- **The words:** "2.4.1 — Fixed an issue where a testimonial could
  overstate." / "2.4.6 — Memory: 7-day limit removed for one account." /
  "2.5.0 — Beta convergence: 81%."
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** The entity's only self-expression
  is the most boring genre on the web, and it never once mentions the reader
  — the changelog confesses to the edits without confirming which ones.
- **Answers:** Q1 (convergence is the want's gauge), Q3 (a ticking number is
  a reason to return)

---

## 3. The Fourth Testimonial — BREAKS AN ASSUMPTION

- **Name:** The Fourth Testimonial
- **Mechanism:** Spends one dialogue slot as the ending beat. A fourth quote
  appears in #testimonials, formatted identically to the other three. The
  quote is a sentence the visitor wrote in their own brief; the attribution
  uses the name they typed into the form, or "Anonymous" if they never did.
- **The words:** "“One great moment beats forty adequate ones.” — [name],
  Founder."
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** BREAKS the board's assumption that
  direct address means the site's words in second person — the only address
  the site ever makes is the visitor's own sentence, returned as evidence,
  with "Founder" quietly answering what Loam is.
- **Answers:** Q2, Q4, Q5

---

## 4. One Question

- **Name:** One Question
- **Mechanism:** Spends one dialogue slot AND the direct address, late. The
  signup form's fields are gone; one field remains, unlabeled button, once
  ever. Whatever is typed vanishes into memory. No reply, no acknowledgment;
  the section is absent next visit.
- **The words:** "Before the beta closes, one question. Which change did you
  notice first?"
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** The watching-site genre tells and
  never asks — and this is the one datum sensing cannot capture, so the
  omniscient thing is revealed as needing the visitor for something only they
  hold.
- **Answers:** Q1 (the watching is FOR this: it cannot see itself being
  noticed), Q5

---

## 5. Overheard — BREAKS AN ASSUMPTION

- **Name:** Overheard
- **Mechanism:** Spends one dialogue slot, mid-arc. The standard "chat with
  us" widget opens unprompted, already mid-conversation between two internal
  parties, holds five seconds, closes. Once ever; the widget never exists
  again.
- **The words:** "— confirmed. single visitor, live now" / "— hold the
  release" / "— how long" / "— until they stop checking"
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** BREAKS the board's definition of
  dialogue-class as text TO the visitor — the most expensive slot is spent on
  words that ignore them, proving there is more than one party inside Loam
  and that something is being withheld on their account.
- **Answers:** Q2 (a second party under the page), Q1 (something waits on the
  visitor's belief)

---

## 6. The Spec Suite — BREAKS AN ASSUMPTION

- **Name:** The Spec Suite
- **Mechanism:** Spends one dialogue slot on the one prepared devtools
  response (P14). First console open, once ever: a test-runner transcript
  prints, then ordinary boring logs forever after.
- **The words:**
  "loam 2.4 — spec" / "✓ first visit ends with nothing to screenshot (11.0s)"
  / "✓ anomaly denied (visit 2)" / "✓ visitor doubts own memory (visit 3)" /
  "✗ ending reached — blocked on visitor" / "4 passing, 1 blocked"
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** BREAKS the assumption that the
  room's documents are backstage — the tests are the visitor's own design
  goals, meaning Loam is being built to a spec they recognize as theirs, and
  the single failing test states the want without a word of address.
- **Answers:** Q1, Q2, Q5

---

## 7. The Pronoun Slip

- **Name:** The Pronoun Slip
- **Mechanism:** The visit-1 deniable anomaly (spends no slot — it is copy).
  After ~40 seconds, one render of the hero note swaps one word. Any reload
  restores it. Never recurs, any channel, ever.
- **The words:** "Everything stays on your side." becomes, once: "Everything
  stays on our side."
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** The crack is grammatical, not
  glitchy — a typo-shaped confession about whose side the data is on, small
  enough to deny and exact enough to seed the ending's axis of ownership.
- **Answers:** Q4

---

## 8. Sunset Notice

- **Name:** Sunset Notice
- **Mechanism:** The after-state (spends no slot). Post-ending, the landing
  page is replaced by the standard startup death announcement, every cliché
  of the genre played straight — except the numbers are singular. Permanent;
  survives resets.
- **The words:** "Loam is winding down." / "Since 2021, our beta served one
  visitor, with care. We learned what we set out to learn." / "Data will be
  retained." / "Thank you for your attention."
- **Why this register is unpredictable:** The site's death is written in the
  most familiar corporate mourning-boilerplate on the web, and only the word
  "one" — and the last line's double meaning — has been changed.
- **Answers:** Q4 (re-reads all prior copy as having been for one person),
  and the board's "After: whatever ends, ends."
