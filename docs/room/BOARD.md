# THE BOARD — v2 series bible (showrunner, Phase 0)

Read BRIEF.md and RESEARCH-fourthwall.md before pitching. This page is the
structure you pitch INTO. It is deliberately incomplete: the open questions
at the bottom are the room's actual assignment.

## What v2 is

An *editing* site, not a talking one. The sensing layer stays; the surfacing
layer inverts. Well over 95% of everything sensed feeds memory and nothing
else. The site proves it noticed by changing itself — between glances,
between visits — and never says so.

## The emotional shape of the whole run (across visits, not minutes)

- **Visit 1:** a genuinely good, genuinely funny-deadpan fake startup page.
  Clean. At most ONE deniable anomaly (bug-shaped, checkable, gone on
  recheck). The visitor leaves with nothing they could screenshot.
- **Early returns:** the site is different than they remember — small,
  specific, unacknowledged. Doubt is the product: "was it always like that?"
- **Middle:** a second frame becomes findable (not announced) by visitors who
  investigate. The site turns out to want something; the want is legible
  through behavior only.
- **Late:** one boundary-crossing out of the page into the browser's real
  chrome (clipboard OR title OR notification OR history — ONE, once). One
  direct address, at most, ever — and it must say something only this site
  could say to this person.
- **After:** whatever ends, ends. The site visibly moves on. Nothing reruns.

## Hard budgets (authored rarity — pitch within these, don't filter later)

- Dialogue-class moments (any modal/bubble/overlay text TO the visitor):
  **≤5 across the entire arc, lifetime.** Zero in the first two visits.
- Boundary crossings into real browser chrome: **1**, late, earned.
- Direct second-person address: **≤1**, later than feels safe.
- Per-session visible anomalies in early visits: **≤1** — and a session where
  the count is 0 is a valid, often better, authored choice.
- Once-flags are sacred and survive every reset the visitor can perform.

## Tone laws

- The Loam page is played completely straight and must be GOOD — the humor is
  deadpan corporate satire that could pass as real. The frame is worth
  breaking or nothing else works.
- Banned: melancholy-AI register, smug-narrator register, "I see you"-ism,
  glitch aesthetics, zalgo, jump anything, sentience confessions, wall-of-text
  lore, emoji, exclamation marks. Wrongness looks *almost right*.
- Comedy is the anesthetic; dread is the incision. Both, or neither works.

## Standing constraints

- Mobile-first (phone at 11pm is the canonical session). Client-side only.
- The v1 engine (event bus, rules, memory channels, phase machine, spaces) is
  the substrate; pitches must be buildable on it or state what small engine
  capability they need.
- The audience is ONE person, who invented the premise and wrote the brief.
  "It watches you" is frame zero — worthless as a twist, priceless as a given.

## OPEN STRUCTURAL QUESTIONS (pitch answers to these)

- **Q1 — The want.** What is the watching FOR? The site needs a want the
  visitor's behavior can help or threaten (not "please stay" — v1 spent that
  and it was cheap). What does this site need from its one visitor?
- **Q2 — The frames.** What is under the landing page, and what is under
  THAT? At least two collapses past frame zero. What is Loam actually?
- **Q3 — The clock.** The arc runs on return visits we cannot force. What
  makes visit 2 happen? What structure survives a visitor who binges in one
  night vs. one who drifts back over weeks?
- **Q4 — The ending seed.** The final beat must recontextualize the ordinary
  copy (testimonials, pricing, the form). What must the ordinary page be
  seeded with NOW so the ending can re-read it?
- **Q5 — The author in the room.** The visitor wrote the design brief. How is
  that dramatic irony USED — concretely, mechanically — without being cute?
