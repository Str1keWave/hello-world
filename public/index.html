What Loam measures

Attention mapping

See where visitors slow down, where they skim, and where they stop reading entirely — paragraph by paragraph.

Hesitation signals

Typing pauses, abandoned form fields, deleted text. The things people almost said are data too.

Return-visit memory

Loam remembers every visitor across sessions, so your product can greet them like it knows them. Because it does.

Retention modeling

Our engagement model learns what makes each visitor stay — and tells you before they decide to leave.
